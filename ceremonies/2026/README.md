# 2026 Cross-Sign and CRL Ceremony

The 2026 ceremony includes:

* The creation of three cross-signs, nearly identical to the cross-signs
  produced in the 2025 ceremony, except containing an EKU extension.
* The creation of two CRLs (from ISRG Root X1 and X2), revoking the
  cross-signs issued in 2025.
* The creation of two empty CRLs (from Root YE and YR), just to keep our CRL
  timings in sync.
